## Working baseline

Craft v1 is the selected experimental profile, served by the pinned Qwen3.5-4B MLX base with the craft-training-v2 adapter. See [configuration](configs/narratryx-craft-v1.json). The v42 matched base comparison found a 4/6 mechanical tie between untuned Qwen, craft and Gemma. The 24-case originality holdout remains reserved. [Base comparison](docs/base-comparison-v42.md)

The v48 source-only run completed 58 updates and 8,863 supervised tokens. Both evaluation phases stopped on dialogue truncation. It returned two of three fresh rewrite inputs unchanged. Craft remains selected. No human review is required for screening. [v48 results](docs/source-training-v48.md)

## Priority backlog

1. Test completion of permitted actions and preserve uncertainty in longer scenes. Compare explicit timing anchors with ordinary scene instructions and keep scoring prose quality separately.
2. Expand the verified James, Shelley and Wilde reserve with examples of indirect emotion and evasion across more scene situations. Keep plot changes in a separate task and reserve fresh prompts for the next candidate.
3. Use the v59 dialogue failures to source lessons in indirect negotiation, distinct speech and clear agreements. Keep the exposed diagnostics and originality holdout separate.
4. Measure runtime on supported platforms before integration. Keep research isolated from the editor until a candidate earns a trial.

## Blockers and limits

Muse equivalence remains unmeasured. No matched Muse responses are available. The current 4B model and limited source curriculum repeatedly trade gains in structured checks for regressions in prose. Avoid another broad mixture sweep until targeted evidence justifies it.

## Milestone: dialogue stability and revision diagnosis

A controlled MLX probe ran four dialogue briefs at penalties 1.0 and 1.12 with seed 42 and temperature zero. Completion rose from 2/4 to 4/4 and turn-limit compliance from 0/4 to 3/4. Two prompts were selected for their prior failures, and the other two had been frozen before training. The penalty improved decoding stability in this sample, but the basket request still switched objects and the music exchange still asserted the copy was faded.

A separate paired rewrite probe added an explicit sentence-structure instruction to two briefs v48 had previously copied. Changed outputs rose from 0/2 to 2/2. The affection rewrite inferred rain and implied a return after boiling, where the original only establishes absence before boiling. The pacing rewrite retained the basic state. These are exposed diagnostics with informed editorial observations. Craft remains selected.

Dickens is separately classified as structural revision. More task-specific examples from other authors are needed before another broad run. The next milestone tests a separate dialogue curriculum with exact turn counts and prepares a stylistic revision reserve. Keep the penalty as an experimental condition alongside the original setting.
