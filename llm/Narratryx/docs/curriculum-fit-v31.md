# Craft commentary fit review

Reviewed all 20 previously scoped Wharton and Hamilton lessons. The review proposal adds six to the original 34-example source curriculum and retains the 16 exposed monitors. It uses the original briefs from v24 because the v29 brief changes gave no continuation gain in their comparison. The six additions are unmodified published answers previously used in craft training.

| Source | Selected skills |
|---|---|
| Wharton, three passages | Viewpoint and perception, dialogue placement and narrative time, story scale |
| Hamilton, three passages | Character/action/setting, an illustrated example of limited viewpoint, ironic emphasis |

The other 14 are reserved. They include additional variations on omniscience, narrator choice, epistolary form, and style. The six selected cover a wider set of topics without letting the two craft manuals dominate this stage of the mixture. Exact source scopes, non-overlap, GB/US training permissions, and the dataset split checks passed. The resulting 40 examples are a review proposal.

All 20 lessons ask about a critic's explanation. Their value is in teaching critical vocabulary and craft principles. None directly trains the fresh screen's task of reading a supplied fictional passage, quoting its evidence and qualifying an inference. The closest example is Hamilton's comparison of viewpoints in adjacent locked rooms. It illustrates the concept without requiring independent close reading of a supplied story.

For the next source search, prioritize historical criticism where the original author quotes or points to precise details in a work and draws a supported inference. Poe's 1846 essay on his composition of *The Raven* is a discovery lead for linking textual details to craft choices. It is an author account about a poem and cannot cover the full fiction analysis task. Woolf's *The Common Reader* offers potential fiction examples, but quoted third-party material and the exact digital edition need separate checks. Both leads remain outside the proposal pending edition and span review.
