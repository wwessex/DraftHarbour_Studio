# Lower-rate writing stability ablation — 1 November 2026

The user authorised the small stability experiment proposed after [v9's failed writing evaluation](balanced-fiction-v9-2026-11-01.md). This trial keeps the balanced training mixture fixed and changes **only learning rate:2e-4→5e-5** in the executable request. It tests whether less adaptation avoids the observed dialogue degeneration while retaining some grounding gains. No app defaults, selected model, prior artifacts or uploaded model assets change.

## Frozen training and scope

Training starts fresh from the same cached Qwen3.5-4B MLX 8-bit base, not v9's adapter. The same 92 unique lessons,128 presentations,56 source-prose lessons,12 focused claim lessons repeated four times,16 exposed Wray monitor records and 10,173 supervised-token exposures remain. Token files, masks, target text, source spans, order/sampling seeds and every other request field are byte-identical or unchanged as applicable. Repetition does not create new literary diversity. No generated literary teaching targets, teacher-model answers, new books, manuscripts or rights grants are added.

The recipe retains LoRA rank 8/alpha 16/dropout 0, seed 42, batch 1/accumulation 1,128 updates, one epoch, two warmup updates, linear schedule and 2,048-token context. Lower rate is the sole changed request key; a tested consistency check verifies that before loading weights. Installed package versions must match the recorded v9 reference environment. Model/base/tokenizer hashes and the project inference-engine source are pinned. Exact project hashes/package-version equality do not establish bitwise equivalence of every aspect of the whole system.

The 10 GB MLX/process-tree memory gates,20-minute training deadline,128-update cap and warning/error/nonfinite gates remain. No paid/cloud job, corpus upload, publication, automatic promotion or application integration is launched. The existing exact per-span/per-split GB/US rights validation and held-out author/work boundaries are preserved.

## Exposed small-suite comparison

The suite has **24 exposed, failure-enriched regression cases**: eight dialogue/continuation/scene-writing cases first, then six clock states, six composite claims and four exact selected-word repairs. It includes the failed coat dialogue and repetitive reel dialogue, plus two writing cases the v9 candidate never reached. The six state positions per structured task are selected by a predefined rule covering future absence, past absence, past unknown, due now, future unknown and known arrival across both schedule families—not by cherry-picking prior correct answers.

The prompts are unchanged from v9. Their original base/current responses are reused, explicitly **not fresh inference**, after verifying prompt hashes, original protocol/source-response hashes, recorded package versions, frozen inference source and unchanged base/current adapters. The high-rate candidate reference has only 22 of these 24 responses; its two missing cases remain missing. Its truncated coat response remains a failure, never silently repaired. A reference audit passes before training. Reuse saves local inference work but these are not independent new tests or broad literary-quality evidence.

New candidate requests retain temperature 0, seed 42, repetition penalty 1.0,384-token output cap, one-message fresh contexts and zero reported cached tokens. This does **not** carry over the post-hoc 1.1 penalty probe. Request error, context overflow or output truncation stops evaluation. A separate exact-repetition proxy flags a long dialogue body of at least six words appearing twice, ignoring speaker labels, outer quotes, whitespace/case and short acknowledgements. Flags are nonfatal quality diagnostics; a run can complete while failing them. Deliberate rhetorical repetition may be legitimate, so this is not an automated literary judgement.

If all requests complete, the procedural stability gate requires no truncation and no such repetition flags. Word constraints, grounding and editing scores are reported separately; that gate is not approval of scene quality, factual fidelity or prose style. No numeric proxy automatically selects a model.

## Results

**Training and all 24 new candidate requests completed; the stability gate failed.** Both dialogue cases contain repeated long utterances. No response reached the output cap. The training phase took 261.13 seconds including setup/save (worker-reported elapsed 233.59 seconds), with reported peak MLX memory 9.86 GB. All 128 updates and 10,173 supervised-token exposures completed without recorded warnings or errors. Exposed monitor loss was 1.0275; this is not independent quality evidence.

## Review and limits

The [blinded writing review](../artifacts/stability-v10/REVIEW.md) compares the current craft reference with completed lower-rate writing responses, using independently randomised A/B labels. Up to eight writing cases support 16 blank ballots for two reviewers; the private condition key is mode 0600. Truncated or unavailable candidate responses are not presented as completed prose. The higher-rate reference is available separately in the objective comparison, including its missing cases and failure. Human literary-quality ratings remain zero.

An informed AI inspection of the new candidate outputs finds persistent substantive defects:

- Coat dialogue has eight turns instead of four to six, repeating Cora's refusal four times. Reel dialogue also has eight turns, repeating a refusal and demand three times each. Both stay under 100 words while failing the practical-negotiation instruction.
- Reel scene writing repeats the same stakes and actions, reaches 153 words against 90–130 requested, and incorrectly equates withholding the reel with leaving the repaired recorder broken. Coat scene writing reaches 139 words and repeats the final choice.
- Reel continuation invents that Anika's recorder is the only machine capable of playback. Rope continuation asserts “It was not Sita” despite the object's unknown origin. Conservatory continuation is only 16 words against 60–90 requested.
- Coat continuation supplies a new permission to use the lining rather than testing a practical next step while preserving the missing-consent constraint.

These observations are attributed to AI inspection after seeing model conditions, not independent blinded human ratings. They show why passing length or exact-repair checks is insufficient. The packet contains eight paired writing cases and 16 blank human ballots; no reviewer scores have been fabricated.

### Next development step

Keep the selected craft model. Before another training job, audit whether the existing source-derived answers actually demonstrate each requested writing operation, especially negotiation, continuation-only output and restrained revision. Quarantine mismatched or repetitive lessons instead of merely repeating more structural labels. Prepare a smaller, task-matched source-only mixture and separate untouched writing cases before choosing another bounded experiment. Any newly acquired editions or spans require their own scoped rights evidence; this report neither clears new sources nor authorises generated literary teaching targets. Further adapter updates should follow that audit, not an automatic retry of this recipe.
