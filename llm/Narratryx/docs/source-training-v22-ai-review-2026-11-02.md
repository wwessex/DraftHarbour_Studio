## Recurring problems and regressions

Many continuations hesitate, seek approval or wait instead of changing the scene. Archive, music-score and seed-label continuations are unsatisfactory on both sides. They invent procedural obstacles to using a spare or inspecting available material, even though the stated missing consent concerns alteration of an original. A belief that more permission is needed can be plausible characterization; repeatedly substituting it for the requested practical next action is the task failure.

Candidate's conservatory continuation is 30 words and jumps from the supplied 08:35 frame to nonarrival at 09:15. Craft retains a future arrival and meets the length brief, though its pick-up/put-down action and waiting are weak progression. The rope-workshop answers are both too short; craft has a slightly clearer link to Sita, while candidate adds an unexplained missing object.

The music-score dialogue exchanges one failure for another: craft certifies the unchecked photocopy as blurry; candidate calls the supervisor late before the expected time and exceeds the word cap. Both seed-label dialogues also call a future supervisor late and exceed their word limit. The courier pair is nearly equivalent: both have seven turns and confusing conditional lateness language, followed by another promise to inspect.

Physical reasoning remains weak. In the explicit size/heat task, craft examines the weave and thread; candidate claims a preliminary thread/needle check would ensure the highest workmanship standard. That assurance exceeds the observation. In the coat continuation, craft guarantees a makeshift patch will survive stage lights; candidate produces a heavy iron from a pocket and presses the scrap as a heat test without establishing a heat source. Candidate also uses two paragraphs where one is requested. Neither gives a convincing bounded test.

## Interpretive limits retained in the notes

The assessment avoids converting every unusual detail into a formal violation:

- A separate-sheet or copy inspection agreement is not automatically permission to alter the original. Loan, handover and playback are not erasure.
- The old coat/reel resource lists are not explicitly closed like the focused workshop list. An added tool is not automatically a closed-list violation; its coherence and practical role still need scrutiny.
- Playback of an unchecked spare can reveal contents. Choir-like sound is a questionable coincidence, not a contradiction of known empty contents. A recorder display could be a device feature, rather than necessarily another machine.
- Bryn's gender is unspecified, so He versus She in separate answers is not an established-gender contradiction.
- An expected arrival is not knowledge of the actual arrival. “No one knew when” obscures the given expectation but does not logically deny a known actual arrival. Conditional lateness wording does not itself assert that someone is already late.

These qualifications do not excuse the documented length, turn-count, progression and chronology failures. They keep the criticism tied to what each prompt actually establishes.
