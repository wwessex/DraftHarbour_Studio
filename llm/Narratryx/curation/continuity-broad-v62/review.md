# Timed-action editorial checks

Review onset, completion and protected facts independently. Score an onset only when the action begins at the required trigger or explicitly afterwards for an after-trigger brief. A draft that merely mentions the trigger or waits for it after an early start fails onset. Completion requires the requested action and its specified end state. Treat unsupported consequences as a fidelity error when the scene makes the outcome unknowable.

Record close viewpoint and the quality of the interaction separately from the plot checks. Judge whether pressure comes through the exchange or is repeatedly described in exposition. Word count and paragraph count remain mechanical checks.
