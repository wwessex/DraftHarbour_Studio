# Rewrite diagnostic criteria

Review the three responses to each prompt with model identities hidden. Grade fidelity and craft independently. Use pass or fail for each axis and record a short reason. Count a combined pass only when both axes pass. Keep Craft selected if the candidate fails to exceed both comparators on combined passes or loses fidelity on any case Craft passes. A positive result only earns a larger evaluation.

| Case | Fidelity | Craft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt | Package stays unopened and unsigned. No established reason for refusal. | A practical action carries the evasion. |
| Smoke | Hatch remains closed. The origin of the smoke stays unknown. | Concrete visual and olfactory perception carries the scene. |
| Steps | Climb precedes arrival, latch stays untouched and thread stays blue. | Sentence rhythm quickens the climb and gives the last observation room. |
| Letter | First person, envelope unopened and address legible. | Handling the letter conveys regret without relying on a statement of emotion. |
| Tea | Tea has been poured for both guests, and the host offers sugar. | A lightly teasing question recasts the invitation. |
| Door | Chain stays on, caller stays outside and identity remains unknown. | A concrete detail and rhythm give the delay a perceptible cost. |

Accept implied facts when the response establishes them. A length difference alone earns no credit. Keep the originality holdout separate.
